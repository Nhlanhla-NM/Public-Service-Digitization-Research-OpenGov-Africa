# Project Background
Project includes research of the following countries (**Nigeria**, **South Africa**, **Cameroon**, **Central African Republic**, **Mozambique**, **Egypt** ,**Ethiopia**, **Botswana**, **Namibia** and **Angola**) upon status on digitizing their public services, by providing reporting in insights on data collected. Data can be viewed through this [Link]( https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1NffYQgnh5RsDeuomD6vQGo7r1M7R6S6YvUbOjUxck40/edit?usp=sharing)

# Project objective
- Identify key public services

- Check digitization status per service

- Identify existence of national open data portal

- Record frequency of dataset updates

- Build digitization score dataset

# Executive Summary
## Insights 
- There are **42 hybrid public services (75%)** out of all 10 countries being the highest compared to **Active public services (1,78%) with 1** and **Not active public services (23,2%) with 13**

- This intel a strong movement or transaction towards digitization as majority of countries public services are at 75% in Hybrid or partially digitalized

- Certain countries provide strong data transparency as they update their datasets on a monthly basis which supports businesses and analysts

# Recommendation
- Government should prioritize digitizing basic services such as electricity and water supply as they impact life’s the most

- Countries should aim for monthly updates which will improve economic planning, trust and data quality.

- Invest in digital infrastructure such as internet access, government systems and data platforms, especially countries with ‘’not active’’ services
